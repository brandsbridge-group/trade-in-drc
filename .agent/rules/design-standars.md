Design Rule: Compact Official Design
Principle
The official TradeInDRC application must adhere to a "Compact Official Design" aesthetic. This means maximizing information density without clutter, resembling high-end financial or government portals rather than consumer marketing sites.

Rules
1. Half-Padding Protcol
Aggressively Reduce Padding: Standard padding should be roughly 50% of typical consumer web defaults.
Standard: p-12 -> Official: p-6 or p-8
Standard: gap-8 -> Official: gap-4
Dense Headers: Headers and subheaders should have tighter leading and margins (mb-2 or mb-4 instead of mb-8).
2. Full-Height Sections
Immersive Views: Major sections (Welcome, Feature Highlights, Registration) should try to occupy the full viewport height (min-h-screen) when possible to create distinct "pages" of content.
Split Screens: Use 50/50 split screens for major interaction points (Login, Register) to balance utility (form) with authority (visuals).
3. Sharp & Precise
Radius: All interactive elements must use rounded-sm (2px) or rounded-none. No rounded-xl or rounded-full for containers.
Borders: Use subtle, precise borders (border-slate-200) to define structure.
