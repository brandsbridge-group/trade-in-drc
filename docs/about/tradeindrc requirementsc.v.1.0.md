## **1\. Project Overview**

The *tradeindrc.com* platform will be the **official bilingual (French/English) online trade portal** of the DRC, enabling verified Congolese and international companies to meet, collaborate, and trade.

The portal will serve as a trusted, government-backed **B2B directory and matchmaking platform** where:

* Congolese businesses promote their products and export capacities;

* International buyers find reliable suppliers and distributors;

* Government officials moderate and verify registered companies.

## **Phase Breakdown & Deliverables**

### **Minimum Viable Product (MVP)**

**Objectives:** Launch a fully functional, government-branded directory with verified company profiles, advanced search, and administrative control.
