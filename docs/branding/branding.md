# Branding Guidelines: TradeInDRC

## Brand Identity
**TradeInDRC** is the official, government-backed trade portal for the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The brand identity must convey **Trust**, **Authority**, **Dynamism**, and **Openness**.

## Color Palette (Official)
The palette is derived from the DRC flag, adapted for a modern digital interface.

### Primary Colors
- **DRC Sky Blue**: `#007FFF` (Flag Blue)
  - *Usage*: Main brand color, primary buttons, headers, active states.
  - *Digital Adaptation*: Use a slightly deeper shade `#0066CC` for better text contrast on white.
- **DRC Yellow**: `#F7D618`
  - *Usage*: Accents, "Verified" badges, Call-to-Action highlights (sparingly).
- **DRC Red**: `#CE1021`
  - *Usage*: Alerts, errors, important notices (e.g., "Verification Expired").

### Neutral Colors
- **Background**: `#FFFFFF` (White) and `#F8FAFC` (Slate-50 for faint backgrounds).
- **Text**: `#0F172A` (Slate-900) for headings, `#334155` (Slate-700) for body text.
- **Borders**: `#E2E8F0` (Slate-200).

## Typography
**Primary Font**: `Geist Sans` (or `Inter`)
- **Characteristics**: Clean, Neo-grotesque, highly legible, modern.
- **Weights**:
  - **Bold (700)**: Headings, Buttons.
  - **Medium (500)**: Navigation, Card Titles.
  - **Regular (400)**: Body text.

## Visual Language: "The Sharp Official"
To match the "sharp corners" requirement and government authority:
1.  **Rectilinear Forms**: 0px border radius on everything. No soft curves.
2.  **High Contrast Borders**: Thin, crisp borders to define sections.
3.  **Grid Precision**: Strict alignment to a 4px/8px grid system.
4.  **Minimalist Decoration**: Use subtle geometric patterns (inspired by Congolese textiles or topography) in low opacity as background textures, avoiding clutter.

## Tone of Voice
- **Professional**: "Verified", "Official", "Authorized".
- **Welcoming**: "Connect", "Partner", "Discover".
- **Direct**: "Register", "Search", "Export".
