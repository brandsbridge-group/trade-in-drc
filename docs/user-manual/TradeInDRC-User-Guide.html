About this guide

TradeInDRC connects international buyers, investors and partners with Congolese companies. This guide explains the main tasks for the two types of people who use the platform every day:

  • Company users — businesses (Congolese or international) that create a profile, list products, publish opportunities, send requests and message partners.
  • Administrators — the platform team that reviews requests, verifies companies, approves Premium memberships and keeps the marketplace clean.
Five languages Every page is available in English, French, Turkish, Chinese and Spanish. Use the language switcher in the top navigation bar to change language at any time. Your choice is remembered as you move around the site.

Part A

Company user guide

For businesses using TradeInDRC to gain visibility, find partners and grow in the DRC market.

1Creating an account & signing in

  1. Click Register in the top-right corner.
  2. Enter your email and a password, then confirm. You can also sign in again later with Sign In.
  3. Check your inbox and confirm your email address. Some actions (such as publishing) require a verified email.
Why sign in matters You can browse the marketplace freely without an account. But to send a request, contact a supplier, register a company or request Premium, you must be signed in first. If you try one of these while signed out, the platform takes you to the sign-in page and brings you back afterwards.

2Exploring the marketplace

  • Search — press ⌘K / Ctrl+K, or use the search panel on the home page, to find companies, products, services and opportunities.
  • Companies — browse the directory, filter by sector and location, and open any company to see its profile and contact options.
  • Products — browse listings, filter by sector and category.
  • Opportunities — view current tenders, partner searches and investment opportunities.
  • Market & sectors — explore the DRC's strategic sectors (mining, energy, agriculture, ICT and more) and market information.

3Creating & managing your company profile

  1. Sign in, then open your Dashboard.
  2. Go to Companies and create your company profile: name, sector, description, location and contact details.
  3. Add richer details where available — logo, certifications, markets served, production capacity and languages spoken.
  4. Submit your company for verification. An administrator reviews it and assigns a trust level. Verified companies appear more credible to buyers and partners.
Keep it complete A complete, verified profile with a logo and clear description ranks better in search and earns more trust. You can edit your profile at any time from the dashboard.

4Adding products & services

  1. From the dashboard, open Products (or Services) and choose New.
  2. Fill in the name, description, sector/category and images.
  3. Save. Your listing appears in the public catalogue and in search results.
  4. Edit or remove listings at any time from the same screen.

5Publishing opportunities & tenders

Use opportunities to advertise a tender, a partner search or an investment opportunity.

  1. From the dashboard, open Opportunities → New.
  2. Describe what you are looking for, choose a category, and submit.
  3. The opportunity is reviewed by an administrator before it goes live. You can track its status in your dashboard.
  4. When other businesses respond, you receive their interest through your inbox.

6Submitting a request

The Request page (in the top navigation) is the fastest way to tell the TradeInDRC team exactly what you need — find a local partner, invest, sell, buy, register a company, request a market report, organise a business mission and more.

  1. Open Request from the navigation bar.
  2. Pick the card that matches your need (for example, Find a Local Partner or Invest in the DRC). This pre-selects the request type.
  3. Fill in the form: your name, company, country, email, phone, sector, preferred location, timeline and a detailed message.
  4. Click Submit Business Request. If you are not signed in, you will be asked to sign in first, then returned to the form.
  5. You receive a confirmation, and the TradeInDRC team follows up with you.
What happens next Every request lands in the administrators' Received Requests dashboard, where the team qualifies it, assigns an owner and follows up until it is resolved.

7Messages & inbox

  • When you contact a supplier or someone responds to your opportunity, the conversation appears in your Inbox (in the dashboard).
  • Open a conversation to read and reply.
  • If a message is inappropriate, you can report it; administrators review reports.

8Going Premium

The Pricing page explains the membership options. Premium boosts your visibility, adds a verified premium badge, priority placement, lead capture and more.

PlanWho it's forPrice
Free RegistrationAny company joining the marketplaceFree
Premium — CongoleseCompanies based in the DRC$3,000 / year
Premium — InternationalCompanies based outside the DRC$3,600 / year

Both Premium plans include the same features — the price differs only by company location.

  1. Open Pricing and choose the Premium plan that matches your company.
  2. If you are signed in and own one company, your request is sent for that company. If you own several, you choose which one.
  3. Your request is submitted for review. You will see a Premium Pending Review status on your dashboard.
  4. An administrator approves the request. Once approved, your dashboard shows Premium Active with your plan and renewal date.
Changed your mind? While a Premium request is still pending, you can cancel it from the Premium card on your dashboard, then request a different plan if you wish.

9Your dashboard & account

  • Overview — quick view of your company, Premium status and recent activity.
  • Analytics — see how your profile and listings are performing.
  • Settings — manage your account. If your email is unverified, a reminder banner appears here; verify it to unlock all actions.
Part B

Administrator guide

For the TradeInDRC team. Administrators see an extra Admin Panel link and have access to tools the public never sees.

1Accessing the admin panel

  1. Sign in with an administrator account.
  2. Open the user menu (top-right) and choose Admin Panel, or go directly to the admin area.
  3. The left sidebar lists every admin section: Dashboard, Verifications, Content, Opportunities, Requests, Premium, Data Hub, Companies, Users, Messages, Taxonomy, Analytics and Settings.
Access is protected Only accounts with an administrator role can open the admin panel. Anyone else is redirected to sign in. Administrator rights are granted at the database level, not self-service.

2Received requests

The Requests section is the heart of day-to-day operations — every request submitted from the public Request page lands here.

  • Summary cards show totals at a glance: Total, New, In Progress, Converted, Pending and the overall response rate.
  • The table lists each request with the sender's name, company, country, sector, what they need, status, date and the assigned follow-up owner.
  • Filters let you narrow by search term, sector, country, status, owner or date.

To work a request:

  1. Open the request to read the full details (contact information and the message).
  2. Update its status as you progress it: New In Progress Converted Pending Closed.
  3. Assign a follow-up owner so it is clear who handles it.
  4. Add internal notes as needed. Changes are saved immediately.

3Premium requests

The Premium section lists every company that has requested a Premium membership.

  1. Review the pending requests — each shows the company, who requested it, the plan (Congolese or International), the amount and the date.
  2. Click Approve to grant Premium. The company immediately becomes Premium, with the plan and a one-year renewal date recorded.
  3. Click Reject if the request should not proceed. You can add a note explaining the decision.
Safe by design Only a pending request can be approved, so a request cannot be approved twice or accidentally revived. Companies can never grant themselves Premium — only an administrator can.

4Company verifications

The Verifications section is the queue of companies awaiting review.

  1. Open a company to review its details and any submitted documents.
  2. Approve and assign a trust level, or reject with feedback.
  3. Companies that resubmit appear again in the queue for another review.

5Companies, users & content

  • Companies — view and manage every company on the platform.
  • Users — manage user accounts and roles.
  • Taxonomy — maintain the sectors and categories used across the site.
  • Content & Pages — manage news, resources, help articles and editable page content.
  • Data Hub — manage market data and price information.
  • Settings — control the home page (carousel, featured companies) and other platform options.
  • Analytics — platform-wide usage and performance.

6Moderation & messages

  • Opportunities — review opportunities submitted by companies before they go live; approve or reject each one.
  • Messages — review reported messages and take action to keep conversations safe.

Support

For help, use the Contact page on the platform or the support details published in the site footer. Administrators can manage published contact and help content from the admin Content section.
