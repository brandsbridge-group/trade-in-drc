# Marketplace Landing (source: 1.ai)

## 1. Page identification & purpose

This is the **Marketplace landing page** — the product/supplier discovery hub of Trade in DRC. Evidence: hero heading "Explore the Trade in DRC Marketplace", nav item "Marketplace", and the page's anatomy (search-forward hero, category grid, Featured Products, Meet Verified Suppliers, persistent buying-request form). It is a marketing-led, conversion-oriented landing that funnels two audiences: **buyers** (search products, browse categories, submit a buying request) and **sellers** (register company, list products). The right-rail "Can't Find What You're Looking For?" form makes lead capture a first-class citizen — the page is as much a lead-gen machine as a directory.

Branding note: the design is signed "Power by BrandsBridge Group" (top-right) and "© 2025 BrandsBridge Group. All rights reserved." in the footer — the customer's agency identity, not part of the product brand.

## 2. Layout & grid

Full-width sections stacked top-to-bottom; below the stats bar the body splits into a **~65/35 two-column layout**: main content (left) + sticky-feeling lead-capture rail (right). Density is high — compact cards, tight gutters (~16px equivalents), minimal vertical whitespace between sections. Section headers are small left-aligned bold titles with a "View All →" link right-aligned in red.

Section order:
1. Top navbar (dark navy, logo left, 8 links center, 2 CTAs right)
2. Hero (~180px tall relative to 1170px artboard width) — photo collage background, heading, subcopy, 4-field search bar, 2 pill CTAs
3. Stats bar (dark navy strip, 5 stat items with yellow/gold icons)
4. Left column: "Explore Products by Category" (2 rows × 6 chips = 12 category chips)
5. Left column: "Featured Products" (1 row × 6 product cards)
6. Left column: "Meet Verified Suppliers" (1 row × 6 supplier cards)
7. Left column: two audience banners side-by-side — "For Buyers" (dark navy) / "For Sellers" (light cream)
8. Right column: "Submit Buying Request" form card + "Why Use the Trade in DRC Marketplace?" benefits card (2×3 icon grid)
9. Footer (near-black): logo + tagline, 4 link columns (Quick Links / Resources / About / Support), Stay Connected socials + yellow "Upgrade to Premium →" button
10. Bottom strip (cream): "Trade in DRC – Connecting international business with local opportunities."

## 6. Borders, radii, shadows & effects

- Radius system: small and consistent — ~6–8px on cards, chips, inputs, buttons; ~10px on hero search bar and audience banners. No full-round pills except nav CTAs (slightly rounder).
- Borders: hairline 1px `#E3E6EA` on all cards/chips/inputs; outlined buttons use 1px dark or red stroke.
- Shadows: essentially none — flat design; separation via borders and background tone shifts (white cards on very light gray).
- Gradients/overlays: hero uses a navy gradient overlay (opaque left → transparent right) over the photo collage for text legibility; stats bar is solid.
- No glassmorphism, no blur effects. Dividers: footer uses subtle column spacing, not rules.

## 9. UX assessment

**Works well**
- Search-forward hero with faceted search (product + category + province + supplier type) matches real B2B sourcing behavior and the customer's earlier "search-forward home" request.
- Clear dual-funnel: buyer CTAs (red) vs seller CTAs (register/list) are consistently color-coded; red is reserved for conversion actions.
- Trust stack is strong: stats bar → verified badges → verified-supplier section → benefits card.
- Persistent right-rail buying-request form shortens the lead path dramatically; smart for a market where users may prefer human-brokered sourcing.
- High information density fits the "compact cards, info density" project design principles already on file.

**Risky / needs care**
- **Nav overload**: 8 links + 2 CTAs will not fit at laptop widths; needs consolidation or a mega-menu.
- **Right-rail form on mobile**: the 65/35 split must collapse; form should move below Featured Products or become a floating CTA opening a sheet — otherwise it's an 8-field wall mid-page.
- **Contrast**: yellow-on-navy stats pass, but small gray 10px description text (~#6B7280 on white at tiny sizes) is borderline AA; card metadata should be ≥12px / darkened. White text over the hero photo right side (truck/workers area) lacks overlay — extend the gradient.
- **Two buttons per card** ("View Product" + "Request Quote") × 6 cards = 12 competing CTAs per row; consider making the whole card clickable with one primary action.
- Phone field with flag/dial-code adds i18n complexity; "26 Provinces Covered" is a fixed fact, fine, but other stats need live data or become credibility liabilities.
- English-only mockup: FR strings will run ~20–30% longer — category chips and buttons need width tolerance.

## 10. Mapping to TradeInDRC site

- **Primary route**: `/[locale]/products` (the products/marketplace listing) — but this design is really a *marketplace landing*, arguably a redesigned `/[locale]/` homepage or a new `/[locale]/marketplace` landing that fronts products + companies. Given the customer's "search-forward home" memory, treat it as the marketplace hub that the homepage funnels into (or merges with).
- Sub-mappings: "Meet Verified Suppliers" → `/companies`; category chips → `/sectors` or `/products?category=`; "Post a Buying Request" / right-rail form → existing **Request page** (customer batch 2026-06-04, migration 00022); "Upgrade to Premium" → Premium packages page ($3000/$3600); "Register Your Company" → auth modal + dashboard company creation.
- Implementation notes (Tailwind/shadcn):
  - Tokens: extend theme with `navy` (#0B1F3A), `brand-red` (#D8232A), `gold` (#F5B800), `cream` (#FDF6E3); reuse existing DRC palette if defined.
  - Hero search: composite `<form>` using shadcn `Input` + `Select` ×3 in a single rounded container (`flex divide-x rounded-lg bg-white`), red `Button` — wire to existing search/products query params.
  - Stats bar: server component pulling counts from Supabase (products, companies, verified tier via `VerificationTier` constants); fall back to configured minimums, never fake counts.
  - Category chips: map to existing `sectors`/`categories` taxonomy (bilingual columns exist) — 12 chips ≈ current category set; use `Link` from `@/i18n/routing`.
  - Product/supplier cards: extend existing card components; verified check from company verification status; keep 1px border, `rounded-lg`, no shadow per flat style.
  - Right-rail form: reuse the buying-request form from the Request page as a shared component; `lg:grid-cols-[1fr_360px]`, form stacks below content `<lg`; react-hook-form + zod, Sonner toasts (NON_BLOCKING_UX).
  - Motion: per docs/MOTION.md — restrained; ≤300ms section mounts, ≤180ms card hovers (border-color/translate-y-0.5), no carousels.
  - Footer: matches existing footer structure; add "Upgrade to Premium" gold button variant.
  - i18n: every string above needs `en.json` + `fr.json` keys; category labels come from taxonomy translations already implemented.
